Classic Era NPC services and quest starters
Source: https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/tree/master/data/Classic
Source files and SHA256 hashes: CLASSIC_NAVIGATION_DATA.txt.
Imported 2026-10-26. Thanks to Questie contributors and upstream data providers.

These are factual NPC/object names, service flags, faction friendliness,
quest titles/IDs, eligibility fields and known spawn coordinates from Classic
base records. No Questie code or quest prose is bundled. No external addon
is required. No downloaded Lua is executed by the extraction process.
Services include flight masters, trainers, innkeepers, repair NPCs, banks,
auctioneers and mailboxes. Quest starters with unsupported skill/reputation/
special requirements are omitted. Runtime corrections and seasonal changes
are not included. Marker availability depends on level, faction/class and known
quest completion/prerequisite state; these are reference records, not live NPC
detection. Mailbox faction restrictions are not inferred.
