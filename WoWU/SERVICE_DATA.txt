This is a compact extraction of factual service names, flags, faction friendliness
and spawn coordinates; it does not bundle or execute Questie's addon code.
Forever coordinates are adjusted by Questie for Forever map bounds. They assume
inherited NPC world positions; new or relocated Forever services may be missing
or inaccurate. Raw base records are used, without Questie's runtime corrections.
Mailbox faction restrictions are not inferred. Markers are known locations, not
live NPC detection. Native map POIs and flight records supplement these records.
Nearby services discovered through actual interaction are remembered separately.

WoWU v0.2.4: includes repair-flag records. Inn records additionally require an
innkeeper title/name to reject contradictory vendor flags.
