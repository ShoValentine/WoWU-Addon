Classic Era quest navigation facts
Source: https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB
Files: data/Classic/classicQuestDB.lua, classicNpcDB.lua, classicObjectDB.lua,
classicItemDB.lua; support/Zones/areaIdToUiMapId.lua.
Retrieved 2026-10-26. Thanks to Questie contributors and upstream data providers.

This extraction contains quest IDs, objective types and numeric outdoor/city
spawn coordinates only. No Questie code, quest prose or NPC names are bundled.
No external addon is required. Downloaded Lua is parsed as literals, never run.
Base records do not include runtime corrections, seasonal spawns or all special
quest mechanics. At most 16 actual spawn coordinates per entity/map are retained.
These are known spawn locations, not live NPC detection or pathfinding. Instance
aliases are excluded. Missing coordinates are reported, never fabricated.
