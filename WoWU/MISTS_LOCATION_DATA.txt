Mists of Pandaria Classic service locations
Source: https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB
Files: data/MoP/mopNpcDB.lua, data/MoP/mopObjectDB.lua,
support/Zones/MoP/areaIdToUiMapId.lua. Retrieved 2026-10-27.
Thanks to Questie contributors and upstream data providers.

All base service records with known coordinates and friendly-faction data are
extracted for flight masters, trainers, innkeepers, repairs, banks and auctions,
plus known mailbox objects. The Mists NPC flags and area/map mappings are used,
with mapping overrides applied after the base relationships. No Era-coordinate
substitution is used. Runtime corrections, phasing and seasonal spawns are not
included. This is a reference database, not live NPC detection. Mailbox faction
restrictions are not inferred. Only factual names/roles/coordinates are bundled;
downloaded addon code is never executed or included. No external addon required.
