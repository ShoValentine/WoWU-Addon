WoWU location catalogue - imported 2026-10-28

Factual game names, roles, entity IDs and coordinates only. No third-party addon
code, images, authored explanations or executable data is bundled or executed.
No external addon is required. Gathering nodes are deliberately excluded.

Coverage limits:
These are ALL usable base records in the selected source/category extracts, not
a claim to know every location in the game. Missing/unmapped coordinates are
omitted. Runtime corrections, NPC patrol waypoints, phased/event conditions and
completion filters are not imported. Treasures may be quest/event dependent,
already collected, or unavailable in the current phase. Rare markers describe
known locations, never a live spawn. No gathering nodes or spawn timers.
Native map POIs and observed services/sightings continue to supplement the data.
Retail static services are curated MapNotes coverage, not a complete NPC census.
Classic/Forever service imports also include vendors, banks, auctioneers, stable
masters, guild registration, tabard designers and battlemasters under Other map
locations. Chests may include quest-associated containers. No quest spoilers or
drop guarantees are inferred. Multiple coordinates are retained in the database;
the map shows one reference per NPC/category, choosing the closest known point
when viewing the player's current zone. Independent containers retain separate
locations. Nearby native markers take precedence over imported duplicates. Treasures have a separate map filter.
